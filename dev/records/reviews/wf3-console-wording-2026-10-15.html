Decisions behind the wording

  • Separate grid points, realizations, and scenario runs. Twelve lookup rows describe one perturbation point. Stochastic draws are separate, and published collection rows also include baselines. Count actual manifest rows rather than inferring the run total from one displayed grid dimension.
  • Keep the experiment scenario-neutral. WF3 generation is model-independent and is not selected or driven by CMIP6 projections. WF2 is a separate plausibility overlay. No Wflow simulation or hydrological performance assessment occurs here.
  • Distinguish readiness from reservation. Snapshot and claim steps precede the generation DAG for a new collection. A claim is not publication. A ready-collection reuse branch can return without launching generation; do not present both branches as one executed transcript.
  • Preserve the transformation path. Unperturbed baselines still use the provider transformation path. Basin cells determine resampling, while retained grids can include surrounding cells needed for downstream preparation. Neither point implies unchanged historical daily series.
  • Keep write timing honest. “Writing” and “moving” describe work about to start; “written” or “ready” belongs only after success. Generator diagnostic figures are not evidence of local hydrological calibration or system-model validation.
