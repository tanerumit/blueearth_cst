## Notes on the disposition pattern

**Zero rejections is a result, not a default.** Every finding was checked against
the repository before disposition, and the code confirmed each one that made a
claim about code — `validate_wg5`'s prefix selector, `check_baseline`'s structural
comparator, `check_not_frozen`'s freeze and its `has_run_successfully` gate, rule
3.15's static output lists, `_category_month`'s single global call and its
first-column collapse, and `validate_wg5_catalog_grid`'s `range(0, st_num + 1)`.
Manufacturing a rejection for balance would have been the dishonest move; the one
place the design pushes back is inside `risk-2`, on the acyclicity rule, and that
is recorded in its row rather than promoted to a disposition it does not deserve.

**One deferral, marked as one.** `domain-7` — fit uncertainty across the seam —
is right and is not closed. §5.4.4a states the position and the route that stays
open; the gap remains.

**Three severity divergences preserved.** `arch-6`/`risk-11`, `arch-7`/`risk-8`
and `risk-1`/`arch-8` each appear twice, at their own filed severities, with
different resolution text, because they are different halves of one defect and the
index records the divergence as deliberate.

**Four things this revision changed that no finding named**, listed so the
external round can see they are not smuggled: grain was already a declared surface
(`METRIC_CLASSES`, `metric_grain`), which reframes §5.4.1 as a refinement and adds
three migration targets; the one-table-one-family rule; the pairing-consistency
refusal; and Goal 1's wording, which was unachievable by any design. The last
three come from `candidate-family-schema.md`.
