## Findings

### ext1-1  [major]
- section: 5. Numerical contracts, items 7–8; 4. Figure contract; 9.2 Checks
- finding: The specified `sqrt(F(28,28))` band does not support all the variability products or the caption claim that ratios inside it are “indistinguishable from sampling noise.” It describes independent Gaussian residual SD estimates under the stated white-noise assumptions. A precipitation CV ratio also contains the ratio of estimated means, whose uncertainty is omitted. Joined near-term windows can overlap the reference window, violating independence. The same band is also displayed against model geometric means and pooled summaries, whose sampling distributions differ from a single-member SD ratio.
- rationale: An unchanged precipitation CV can fall outside the advertised band because of mean-estimation uncertainty, while overlapping windows or member averaging can produce substantially narrower null distributions. Users therefore receive an inconsistent sampling benchmark across panels. The proposed stationary-series test does not explicitly test CV ratios, overlapping windows, or the displayed model summaries, so it cannot falsify these errors.
- suggested_fix: Retain the required no-change sampling band, but define its applicable statistic and sampling unit for each panel. Provide a justified CV-specific null calculation, handle overlapping windows, and distinguish single-member bands from model-summary uncertainty. If an illustrative white-noise SD benchmark is retained, label it as such and remove the “indistinguishable” inference. Add null simulations for every statistic and window relationship to which the band is applied.

### ext1-2  [major]
- section: 6. Robustness and availability; 6.1 Declared vs written, without placeholders
- finding: Runtime unavailability contradicts the promised user journey. Section 6 permits missing historical runs, incomplete member windows, and absent analysis references, with unavailable rows and reasons. Section 6.1 instead fails the entire preparation rule when these exclusions leave a parse-time-declared product empty. Availability derived from `POINTS` and nominal acquisition spans cannot establish usable observations, valid ratios, or paired precipitation–temperature cloud points.
- rationale: A legitimate ensemble can produce valid configured change factors and several useful analysis products yet fail WF2 because one optional figure becomes undrawable. The failure also prevents delivery of the completed availability record and “Not produced” captions promised by the settled framing.
- suggested_fix: Distinguish corrupt inputs from legitimate product unavailability. Determine final product availability after inspecting and validating the series, and let rendering consume that result without requiring nonexistent images. Specify a concrete Snakemake output boundary that supports this behavior, such as an owned figure directory plus a manifest, or a checkpoint. Test an ensemble with some usable products and one runtime-unavailable product.

### ext1-3  [major]
- section: 5. Numerical contracts, item 4; 2. Module boundaries; 9.2 Checks
- finding: The mean-keyed dry rule leaves zero reference variability undefined without a specified handling contract. A wet, constant precipitation series passes the dry threshold but has zero SD and CV; a constant temperature series likewise has zero detrended SD. Restoring an SD percentage change or computing future/reference variability ratios then divides by zero. Zero future variability also produces a zero ratio that cannot enter the specified log-scale quantiles or geometric means.
- rationale: These inputs can yield infinities, undefined logarithms, or inconsistent member exclusions despite being valid climate series. Consequently, model counts, pooled weights, and variability figures can become invalid or disagree without an explanatory status.
- suggested_fix: Define separate statuses and inclusion rules for zero reference variability, zero future variability, and insufficient residual observations. Preserve valid absolute changes, avoid silently inserting numerical floors, and specify whether boundary ratios are reported separately or excluded from log summaries. Recalculate weights over eligible members and add constant-series and zero-future-variability tests.

### ext1-4  [major]
- section: 2. Module boundaries; 5. Numerical contracts; 9. Validation plan
- finding: The new seasonal calculation specifies DJF/MAM/JJA/SON and month-length weighting but does not define season-year labels, boundary handling, or completeness requirements. Thirty complete water years do not necessarily contain thirty complete instances of every calendar season. For January water years, DJF crosses the analysis-window boundary; other water-year starts create analogous boundary cases.
- rationale: Implementations can produce different seasonal means, variability estimates, and sample counts by dropping boundary seasons, including months outside the recorded window, or accepting partial seasons. Lab parity on a single configuration can reproduce the prototype convention without establishing that the stated window contract is satisfied.
- suggested_fix: Specify season-year ownership, required months, treatment of boundary seasons and missing months, and the resulting sample-count contract. State explicitly whether seasonal calculations may access months outside the analysis window. Add hand-calculated DJF cases for January and October water years, including missing boundary months and calendar-dependent month lengths.
